WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
// This is what no other marketplace in Uganda does.
🏬
Shop-First Discovery
Most marketplaces show you products. Garuga shows you SHOPS.

User selects a category → they see shops near them, not random products.
This mirrors how Garuga road actually works in real life — you trust a shop, not a product on the ground.

Nearest shops appear first. Verified shops get their own highlighted section at the top.
📍
Location-First
Location required on entry. Every result is proximity-based.
Hyperlocal by design — built for Garuga road first, then all of Entebbe.
No one else is doing this for this market.
✅
Verification Badge Psychology
Unverified shops are still visible — we don't lock anyone out.
But verified shops sit in a premium section above the rest.
Sellers feel the difference. They upgrade without being forced.
That's how you monetize without killing the community.
