How to Approach
// The exact steps to walk in and close a partner.
✅ Always Do
  • Ask specifically for the owner — not staff
  • Go in the morning, slow hours
  • Show the live platform on your phone
  • Keep the pitch to one sentence
  • Start with people you know personally
  • Let them sign up on the spot
✗ Never Do
  • Talk to staff — they'll say no automatically
  • Go during busy peak hours
  • Bring a pitch document or brochure
  • Ask for money upfront
  • Approach more than 3 at once — focus
  • Promise features you haven't built yet
01
List 10 Shops You Know
Warm connections convert 3x faster than cold walks. Write down 10 specific business owners on Garuga road you know personally. Start there before approaching strangers.
02
Walk In — Morning Only
Weekday mornings 8am–11am. Owners are present, not busy. Afternoons they're rushed. Evenings they're tired. Morning is the only time you get a real conversation.
03
Show — Don't Tell
Open garuga-marketplace.web.app on your phone. Show them existing listings. Show them the categories. A live product beats any explanation. Let them see it working.
04
One Sentence Pitch
"I list your products on Garuga Marketplace, customers order online, you pay me a small commission only when you make a sale. No upfront cost." Then stop talking.
05
Sign Up On the Spot
If they say yes, list their first product right there with them. Don't leave and say "I'll add you later." The moment you walk out without listing, the deal is 50% dead.
06
WhatsApp Follow Up
Get their WhatsApp number. Send them the link to their listing same day. People who see their own shop live become invested. That's your retention hook.
The Pitch
// Exactly what to say. Nothing more.
Universal One-Liner
"I list your products on Garuga Marketplace, customers order online, you pay me a small commission only when you make a sale. No upfront cost."
Then stop talking. Let them respond. Silence after a pitch is not awkward — it's the moment they decide. Don't fill it.
For Delivery Partners (Bodas)
"When an order comes in on Garuga Marketplace near you, I send it to you. You deliver it. I pay you per drop. You keep riding — just add deliveries on top."
Bodas respond to immediate income. No monthly commitment. Per delivery. That's the hook.
If They Ask "How Does It Work?"
"Customer sees your product, taps order, you get notified, you confirm, boda delivers or they pick up. You get paid directly — I just take my cut."
Keep it simple. They don't need to understand technology. They need to understand money.
If They Say "I'll Think About It"
"No problem. Let me just list one product for you now for free — if you don't like it I remove it. Nothing to lose."
Get something listed. A free first listing converts skeptics into believers faster than any conversation.
